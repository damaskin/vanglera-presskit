VANGLERA — BIO (EN)

VANGLERA is a DJ exploring sound at the crossroads of Sexy Groove, Hypnotic Techno and Dark Rave.

Her musical language is shaped by hypnotic rhythms, complex sonic textures and an experimental sound. Distorted voices, sensual details, unconventional rhythmic choices and unexpected combinations of elements build an atmosphere of tension, depth and unpredictability — where groove becomes the starting point for sonic experiments.

Her immersion in the underground scene began long before her path as a DJ. Years of devotion to club culture, a trained musical eye and a constant search for new forms of sound shaped her own vision of music and her approach to building sets.

Her decision to step behind the decks was backed by well-known artists and musicians who shared their experience and helped her master the finer points of DJing. Combining that knowledge with her own musical background, VANGLERA went on searching on her own — pushing beyond the familiar and finding new ways to interact with sound.

A deep connection to underground culture and an active presence on social media have let her build a wide circle and an audience of her own. Social platforms became a space that unites people around music, aesthetics and club life, extending that connection far beyond the dancefloor.

VANGLERA is a search for her own musical language through sound, aesthetics and emotion. Constant motion, the courage to experiment and a drive to create an atmosphere where music is felt not only with the ears, but with the whole body.
